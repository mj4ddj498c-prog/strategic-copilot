Role Overview
As VP of People, you carry a dual mandate
We are looking for an executive-caliber People leader to serve as Vice President of People for
Purchasing Power (PPC) and enterprise People Partner Operations across PROG Holdings.
This is a senior, highly visible role for an HR executive who thrives at the intersection of
business strategy, people leadership, and organizational performance. Working with
leadership, your team works closely with all functional leaders and executives at Purchasing
Power and PROG Holdings.
This is the most senior People leader for Purchasing Power — a member of the PPC
leadership team and the architect of its people strategy through and beyond integration into
PROG. Concurrently, you will lead PROG’s enterprise People Partner function: a team of
forward-facing HR consultants embedded across every business unit and function throughout
the enterprise, including operations in India. Also, you will lead Internal Communications across
all PROG Holdings entities.
People Partners are PROG’s primary bridge between the People function and the business.
They sit close to the leaders, teams, and employees they serve — and are directly accountable
for helping the business drive high performance through organizational design, performance
management, talent management, workforce planning, and employee relations. This is
consultative, high-judgment HR work at its best.
This role reports directly to PROG’s Chief People Officer and partners closely with PROG’s
enterprise People COEs spanning Total Rewards, Talent Acquisition, HR Technology,
Learning & Development, Engagement, Community Outreach, and the AI Talent Workstream.

The International Vice President of People and Internal Communications will demonstrate a
passion for managing and developing high performance teams, able to work “scrappy” and
will personally lead a team of 9 employees working in Utah, Michigan, and India. This role will
work closely with the Chief People Officer and the Executive Team.
The position will initiate innovative and proactive approaches in holistic People solutions. This
should be an active champion for diversity and belonging; a driver of programs to attract,
retain, and motivate current and future employees. Strategies will convey a “high touch, high
talent” branded approach in communications, operations, and administration while positioning
the company as a “Great Place to Work.”
The international Vice President People and Internal Communications will help scale it into a
world-class function, implementing a high-performance driven coaching approach for
development of skills and innovation. An entrepreneurial passion and an innovating disruptive
approach are especially important to support this emerging and growing company.

Opportunity Profile
The Vice President of Human Resources is a strategic leader responsible for shaping and executing enterprise -
wide talent strategies that enable the organization’s future workforce needs. This role partners closely with the
Chief People Experience Officer to drive innovation in HR, advance end -to - end talent strategies, and foster a
culture of connection and belonging. The VP will oversee multiple HR functions, including Talent Management,
HRIS, HR Business Partners, Talent Acquisition, and Community & Connec tion.
As the workplace continues to evolve, the VP of HR will play a critical role in guiding the organization through
digital transformation and shaping the future workforce. This includes integrating artificial intelligence (AI) and
advanced technologies into HR practices, building AI -ready capabilities across the enterprise, and ensuring
ethical, responsible use of emerging technologies in partnership with IT. Success is measured by business
outcomes (growth, productivity, quality), workforce health (engagement, retention, skills readiness), and risk
control (compliance, data privacy, regulatory adherence).

Role mandate
This person owns the portfolio of McKesson's strategic HR priorities, including HR transformation and other critical initiatives. The role connects the various projects, dependencies and decisions across HR and makes sure leadership is discussing and deciding the right things at the right time.
It also has responsibility for portfolio budgeting, resource planning and financial performance and oversees program, product and project managers responsible for major HR initiatives.
Key responsibilities
The role is responsible for:
Managing the portfolio of enterprise HR strategic priorities, transformation programs and critical projects, including their interdependencies.
Overseeing portfolio/program budgets, resources and financial performance.
Directing program, product and project managers and partnering with initiative sponsors.
Working with the HR Leadership Team to shape the overall HR vision.
Partnering with Corporate Communications on messaging supporting that vision.
Developing HR strategies aligned with McKesson's broader enterprise strategy.
Establishing an HR governance model across business units and COEs, including the processes and protocols supporting it.
Driving HR innovation and digital capability, including technology-enabled process improvement.
Facilitating enterprise-level strategic planning, budgeting and design-thinking sessions with senior HR leadership.
Leading HR program-management activities and continuing to mature the HR PMO.
Integrating change management and communications into major programs.
Leading and developing a team of program/project-management professionals.
